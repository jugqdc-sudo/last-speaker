# samples

short clips cut from the tape so you can hear what the crew is pointing at.

- `torwali_3845_not_his_usual_voice.mp3` - 38:35-39:00. the loudest register shift on the tape:
  332 Hz against his median 153 Hz. the crew does not know what it is, only that it is not his usual voice.
- `torwali_0218_rule_3_oxo.mp3` - 02:17-02:37. rule 3, the ending `ɒ x ɒ`, one of its 10 occurrences.
